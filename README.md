# 🐀 Ratémon GO: NYC Subway Edition

A Pokémon GO inspired real-world exploration & catching game set entirely in the grimy underworld of New York City!

## 🎮 Features
- **Interactive NYC Map (Leaflet & GPS Sim)**: Explore real NYC spots (Times Square, Union Square, Subway stops, etc.) or click anywhere on the street grid to move your character.
- **12 Unique NYC Ratémon Species**:
  - 🍕 **Pizza Rat** (Manhattan)
  - 🦝 **Sewer Behemoth** (Bronx)
  - 💼 **Wall St. Mole-Rat** (Financial District)
  - ⚡ **Third-Rail Surfer** (Queens)
  - 👑 **Bodega Kingpin** (Brooklyn)
  - 🥾 **Deadass Timbs Rat** (Staten Island)
  - 🥋 **Sensei of Canal St** (Chinatown Legendary)
  - *...and more!*
- **Subway Encounter & Pizza Flinging**:
  - Tap wild rats on the radar to enter the subway battle arena.
  - Fling **🍕 Dollar Slices** or high-tier **🥪 Bacon Egg & Cheeses (B.E.C.)** to feed and capture them.
  - Target ring mechanics, squeaks, and catch rate calculations.
- **Metro Trash Stops (PokéStops)**:
  - Visit and spin city trash cans and dumpster stops to restock your pizza and bait.
- **Full Rat-Dex & Sanitation Profile**:
  - Track caught species, CP ratings, counts, and level up your Exterminator badge.
- **Audio Synthesizer FX & Confetti**:
  - Built-in Web Audio API sound effects (squeaks, pop throws, victory fanfares) and confetti celebrations with no extra setup required.

## 🚀 How to Run

Double-click `index.html` or open it with any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
